Dropping it into Godot

Copy the art/ folder to res://art/. In the import dock set every PNG to Filter: Nearest and Mipmaps: off — one wrong filter setting is the difference between pixel art and mush.

art/thehaunt_terrain.tres is a Godot 4 TileSet with all 55 tiles registered at 16×16 and a physics layer with box collision already on the woods tiles. Assign it to a TileMapLayer and the boundary ring is solid with no code.

The one manual step is the terrain set for the dirt autotile: add a terrain set in the TileSet inspector, then paint the 16 row-1 tiles with their matching peering bits. Two minutes in the editor, and much easier there than in a resource file.

Where this saves you money

Keep, don't replace
Terrain, kerbs, props and the light sprites are good enough to ship a vertical slice with. Spend nothing here. An asset pack purchase is the fallback, not the plan.
Commission narrowly
Characters first — the cast's silhouettes and the walk cycle. That is the one place placeholder quality reads as amateur, and it is a bounded order against the §06 frame table.
Quote against this
Send an artist these PNGs plus the bible. A quote against existing art at a locked palette avoids the revision rounds that make commissions expensive and slow.

Not yet drawn: farm and interior tilesets, crop growth stages, tool-use animations, NPC variants, and the Act II / Act III variant sets. All of those inherit this palette, so they can be added in any order.
