The cast, dressed for now

The town looks like 1958 because nothing new has been built in it. The people don't. Every sprite here wears something you could buy this year, cheap and off a rack: tees, jeans, ball caps, a hoodie, a cardigan, a hi-vis vest, a work apron. No waistcoats, no long aprons, no bonnets.

Spec is unchanged: 16×32 cells, feet on the bottom row, full ink-700 outline, three directions with right mirrored from left, 2-frame idle at 1.5fps and 4-frame walk at 8fps. Nothing in the engine changes — these are file drops.

What Jane wears, and why
A cream tee under an open olive chore shirt, straight jeans, dark boots. Nothing she bought for the farm — this is what a laid-off city woman packs into a trailer, and it happens to be what she grew up working in. Narrow build: shoulders two pixels inside the cell, one narrower than the men, so she reads slighter than the crowd at Billie's.
The ponytail is the silhouette
Auburn, low, and drawn on all three facings — from behind it runs down the jacket, in profile it clears the collar. A player spends most of the game looking at her back. Cream at the chest is the value anchor; the olive keeps her out of a fight with the grass; jeans are her only saturated block.

Rules this pass established

One head shape per character
At 16px wide the head is the only reliable identifier: ponytail, braid, bun, long, mop, cropped, bald, brimmed cap, watch cap, hard hat, hood. Twenty characters, no repeats within a frame.
Two garment colours per torso
A shirt plus one layer — jacket, cardigan, vest, apron or smock — and a shade step. A third colour turns to noise the moment the sprite moves.
No new palette slots
Denim is water-mid/water-deep, hi-vis is lantern, flannel and cardigans are barn-red, auburn is hair-stock with a warm light step. Two slots stay reserved.
Dread accents stay off people
plum, bile-green and bone appear on no garment in Act I, Pell included. The first character to wear one is the reveal.
Two deliberate omissions. The police station and hardware store are cast-empty per canon, so no sprites exist for them. The drive-in has no cast either. Nothing here spends a dread accent, and nothing here draws the pit.
