# Drive-in Movie Theater

This used to be a major place for the town to come together. It shut down some years ago. It obviously shows movies, but also had an arcade, a bar, and a set of stands outside where residents who didn't drive could also watch movies.

For the game, it is a major main story goal to repair and reopen the space. Jane, the main character, starts as a farmer, but needs to buy and rebuild this space. It is what unites the town as a unit. The part of the town that doesn't want to fight the haunt will come to know and trust Jane. The part that does will respect Jane and her strength.

Reopening the theater will restore community and hope.

It starts as closed and run down. There should be a chain blocking the road from the entry map, with a sign stating that the theater is private property and closed. It prevents Jane from entering until summer. In summer, the chain is down from 9am until 6pm. During that time she can enter.

Inside the map at that time is "Shelly". She is a new character. She was born in town, and has been stuck here all of her life. Her parents owned the property, and she now owns it. She lives in a room she rents in town  (we'll sort out where everybody lives later). She comes in summer. Opens the chain, sits in a chair, and reads books. There's not much else to do. She manages to live from the savings she stashed away during her years as a nurse in the clinic. She is 63.

Shelly comes back because she's nostalgic. She holds no hope of the theater reopening, but offers to sell the property to Jane. It would free her from the town, but she has no plans to leave. Where would she go? She is reluctant to sell, because this is her private space. The price is high. Let's say $250k.

After Jane buys the property, Shelly disappears until Jane manages to get the projector units working. From that point, Shelly becomes an ally, and helps Jane in various ways with Jane's other tasks. Details will be added later.

To reopen the theater, Jane needs to:
- buy it
- fix the projectors (this is a multistage task that will be defined later)
- repair the audio system for the cars
- repair the community seating
- restore power to the arcade

Getting the theater running again will require a more advanced level of the planned
mechanical repair skill (Kevin, 2026-08-29 — see the garage next to the gas station in
`docs/story/README.md` and the skills note in `docs/design.md`).

Shelly will fix the arcade games that need repair after power is restored. She grew up with the place, and is rather excited about the prospect of it opening again.

Reopening the theater is a major milestone in game play. It will provide a regular source of income, which will allow Jane to focus on the main mission goal of removing the curse on the town. She will split the revenue with Shelly, and Shelly will run the theater.

Later in the game, something terrible will probably happen to Shelly, and the theater will go dark again, but that's late game, when things get very bleak. TBD.
