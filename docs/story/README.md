# The Haunt

The game takes place in a small isolated town in the north-east US. It is generally not found on maps. Few people outside of the town are really aware that it exists. You can drive into it. It does actually exist, it's just not really known about.

It is only reachable by a side road that is rarely traveled. There are people that come and do deliveries to stock stores, traders that arrive in town selling various things. But otherwise, there is really no tourism to speak of. You either know why you're coming to town, or you stumble on it.

The town itself is nice. The residents work hard to keep it nice. It would be a wonderful place to visit, but the residents have little capacity to advertise, and significant reason not to.

The town has secrets.

The primary secret of the town. If you own a place in town, you can't leave. The town physically won't let you. There is a road to the west and east that lead out of town. If you are a resident and attempt to leave through either, you're simple wrapped around to the other side. That's it.

This fact is known to the residents, and rumored by outsiders. As such, property does occassionally change hands, but due to the remote nature of the town, and rumors, from a practical perspective, the residents are permanent. Nobody knows what happens after selling property and leaving. The few residents who have done so did leave, but they were never heard from again. Whether the town somehow prevents communication, or they simply die, is unknown. This by itself causes most to simply not bother.

Life in town is generally pleasant, and the residents do make the best of things. There are events, shows, social gatherings. It is like any town in a cozy game setting.

But the haunt that stalks the town is not passive.

Periodically, the town demands a sacrifice. Somebody needs to die. It makes these demands known by activating various dangerous entities. Most residents have heard about this as rumor. Few are directly involved. The town is much more likely to make the demand when strangers arrive and stay in town. Often, the people managing the sacrifices will abduct a stranger. Better than sacrificing someone from town, although this does periodically happen.

We'll provide more detail to the lore as time goes on. In summary, though, an evil haunts the town. As the player progresses, they learn more about it. The eventual goal is to attempt to break the curse. The game is structured as a regular cozy town life simulator, but there is an underlying quest behind everything. The long term goal is to create multiple "seasons", like a TV show. After it seems the haunt and curse is lifted, we'll find a deeper underlying issue with the town. Season 1 is to stop the haunt and sacrifices. Season 2 is probably focused on preventing the haunt from escaping. Season 3 maybe has the military come to town and attempt to harness the haunt. Beyond that, we may explore the past, or even the future.

But for now, there's a town and a haunt.

# Main Character

The main character, let's call her Jane, was living in a large city. They were just laid off. They were married, and had a child, but the child died in a tragic fall down a flight of stairs. Their partner blamed them for the death, and suddenly left one day. They had little money, so the divorce was uncomplicated. Jane packed everything she owned into a small moving trailer and decided to drive across country with no clear goal or destination.

Jane grew up on a farm. While she decided long ago that farming wasn't the life she wanted, she missed it. Especially with so much heartache. Jane dreams of a simple life away from the realities she's experienced.

Jane's family eventually lost the farm. After that, her father worked as an auto mechanic. He would bring Jane to work with him, and he taught her everything he knew about cars.

She stumbled onto the town and found it odd that such a nice town wasn't on the map on her phone. She drove through, seeing everything there, then stumbled onto a farm on the north outskirts of town. There was a "For Sale" sign. Old and tattered. It looked like it had been there for years. An old man was there repairing the sign. He had the sun-battered look of an old farmer. She pulled over and struck up a conversation. In summary, he explained that he wanted to move back closer to family, but because the town was remote, he'd had trouble selling the farm. He'd gotten word that his wealthy sister was dying, and he was desperate to get back to her quickly. But he wanted to sell the farm before he left.

His price was too good to be true, but Jane didn't question it. Even if she changed her mind next week, she could surely make a profit. The only demand from the farmer was that they write an agreement on the spot, and she writes him a check. It was pretty much everything she had, but she didn't have much. And a home, land, well that was a lot more than she had currently. She took the chance (after a few glasses of the fantastic wine the farmer claimed was his own brew).

What Jane didn't know was that had sealed her fate. She was now a resident of the town. Bound to the curse. The old farmer promptly left. We'll never hear from him again.

# Locations

## Farm

The farm itself has seen better days. The wine the farmer served seems to have not been made on his farm. The land is certainly fertile. But the barn is run down and empty. The house is livable enough, but sparse. There is a bin to put crops in to be picked up overnight in return for cash. There is a well. The farmer does give Jane a package of seeds to start, and basic tools, but that's it. Jane will need to upgrade this farm over time to make real money.

## West entry

The road from the west. This scene has a motel, a gas station, and a fireworks stand. The motel is where many of the sacrifices will ultimately come from, when they are strangers. It is a shit motel. There is a proprietor who is a middle-aged alcoholic. He is gruff, but steeped in town rumor. An unreliable information source, to be sure, but some of what he has to say will be useful. He doesn't talk much in the morning, is most insightful from about 2pm-5pm, and from there on, he mostly is sad about his lost wife and failing business.

The gas station will have various things for sale that we'll sort out later. Food, of course, but some of the pills and other things that gas stations sell may be useful for missions, but come with side effects. The person working there is a 20-something incel. His conversation is sarcastic, and rarely insightful.

Next to the gas station is a car repair garage. It starts closed and for sale — $100k, for now. Jane will eventually need to buy it. Cars are the trade her father taught her after the family lost the farm, and the garage anchors the skills system (Kevin, 2026-08-30 — the v1 numbers live in design.md §Canon): mechanical repair is one of the skills, Jane will need a more advanced level of it to get the drive-in theater running again, and in the later game she'll need those skills as part of her effort to remove the curse on the town. Once Jane owns it the garage is open 7 days a week, 9am-6pm (Jane herself can get in at any hour), customers drop cars off for repairs — oil changes, lights, transmissions to start — and come back for them on a 2-day deadline, paying the day after the work is done. The clerk is Mike (Kevin's name): friendly, not a mechanic; his only job is taking new customers and collecting the money, and it's his word that reaches Jane when a car comes in.

The fireworks stand will be useful later in game as a source of materials for weapons. Jane can buy stuff now, and set them off, but they won't help a lot. The proprietor is an oddly progressive woman who spent her early years in college fighting to protect abortion rights before Roe v Wade. We find out later she met a guy in town while coming to pick up a large ammount of cannabis (there will be a drug trade in town that is revealed later), and while doing mushrooms, got married. He died years ago. She sells fireworks to get by.

## East entry

Road from the east. Police station. Hardware store. Barber/Hair salon. The police station we'll detail later, but they'll have an important place in the story. The Hardware store starts closed. The owner is in the hospital. The Hair salon is owned and operated by Sam. Gender unclear. Sam only communicates in vaguely poetic statements that mostly rhyme. The player can get their hair styled there, but nothing much happens early game in the hair salon.

## Billie's

East of the west entry. It is a dive bar. Jane is barely welcome there when the game starts, and both the patrons and the proprietor let her know that. It has a handful of scraggly drunks at the bar at all open hours. They do have "shifts", so there's a bunch of drunks that are there when the bar opens. Some leave and are replaced by others in the mid-afternoon. Those are replaced by a handful of evening drunks and a few more ordinary locals. There will be one patron that is there the entire time, at the end of the bar. "Bud". Bud is old and has seen some shit. He moved to town after Vietnam. Bud will have a deeper story later in the game.

Billie is the owner. He walks with a limp and has a patch over one eye. In later game, he will be an ally. He's fought the evils in town, and would prefer to break the curse than simply live with it, but he's a beaten man.

Outside of Billie's, in the same frame, is the pit. It's a deep hole, covered early game. Nobody talks about it. Access is chained off, with a warning sign not to approach it. That's a later story.

## Fork

East of the Billie's frame is a fork in the road. North leads to our farm. East leads to the town center. South will lead to something else later, but it is currently chained off.

## Town Center

Town center should be large. It needs the following:

- Town hall
- Medical clinic
- General store
- "Stumble Inn" - the "nice" bar in town
- About 10 homes for various characters spread around
- A town square and park area with benches, a fountain, and flowers surrounding it

North of town leads to some wilderness. That's where we'll eventually put other areas. Fishing spots, odd characters, cave/dungeon entrances, etc.

South of town is the power station. No details yet.

East of town leads to another fork. On the side of the road there's a homeless person in a shack. Abe. His story is that his car broke down in town 20 years ago while he was also travelling across country. He had a tent and was camping in various towns. He stayed here, and built his shack. He's a bit odd, but as he's never bought property, he's not actually a resident. Technically speaking. He's been useful to various people in town because he can in fact leave. We don't learn much about him early on.

North of the fork is the mansion. It is in ruins. We can't get access early game, but it'll play a significant role later. It is gothic and creepy. The foliage is overgrown.

East of the fork is the east entry.

## Drive-in Movie Theater

This is off the side of the road, to the west of the east entry. It should have a screen, spaces for cars, a seating area for people who came without cars, and an office/consession building. It is not functioning. It shut down years ago. The main character will have a long-running goal of refurbishing and opening the drive-in movie theater.
